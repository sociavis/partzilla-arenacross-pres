Our Story
Built by a Dad,
Built for Racers

What started as a father's frustration watching his sons spend "three or four hundred bucks just for a two-dollar trophy" became the fastest-growing Arenacross championship in America. Bobby Snyder created TriStar to give kids a true pro-level experience — big trophies, podium interviews, holeshot awards, real respect.

YEAR 1
TriStar Indoor Series launches — full-scale AX track inside Cooper Steel Arena. "Guys were blown away. It was a hit right away."
YEAR 2
Pro program added · AMA-sanctioned · East Coast #1 plates · FS1/FS2 broadcast debut. Partzilla joins as title sponsor.
YEAR 3
346 national TV airings · 13.2M Facebook views · Record attendance & entries. The most televised Arenacross series in the country.
"This will be our second year on national TV… We've built something really special."
— Bobby Snyder, TriStar MX
