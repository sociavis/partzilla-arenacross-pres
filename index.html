About the Company
Built for Racers,
by Racers

Bobby Snyder started racing his two young sons at amateur events, frustrated watching kids work so hard for cheap trophies. He created TriStar to give amateur and pro riders alike a professional-caliber experience.

YEAR 1
TriStar Indoor Series launches — drew competitors from 13–15 states. A hit right out of the gate.
YEAR 2
Professional racing added · FS1 broadcast debut. The series gained national exposure and legitimacy.
YEAR 3
Partzilla title sponsorship · AMA East Coast Regional plates. Record attendance, 346 national TV airings, and 13.2M social views.
